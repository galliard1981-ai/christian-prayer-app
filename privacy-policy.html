Privacy Policy

Christian Prayer — Rope n' Rosary  •  Last updated: September 30, 2026

This app ("Christian Prayer — Rope n' Rosary", developed by CultEQ) is a prayer-rope / rosary counting companion for Android. This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

Summary

The app does not require an account, does not collect personal information, and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. All prayer settings and counts are stored only on your own device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers, because we don't operate any servers that the app talks to. The app has no sign-up, no login, and no user accounts.

Information stored on your device

Your prayer-rope preferences (such as bead count, language, sound, vibration and reminder settings) are saved locally on your device only, using standard Android app storage. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Permissions the app uses

  • Vibration — used to give haptic feedback as you move through the beads, and for the optional "remind me if I pause" feature.
  • Notifications — used only for the optional on-device reminder if you pause mid-prayer. No notification is sent from any server; it is generated entirely on your device.
  • Internet — used only if you choose to tap the optional donation link, which opens your browser to an external, independent payment page (see below). The app itself does not send any data over the internet.

Donations

The app may include an optional "Support / Donate" link. Tapping it opens an external website in your browser, operated by a third-party payment provider, to process the donation. We do not receive, process, or store your payment details — that is handled entirely by the third-party provider under its own privacy policy and terms.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
